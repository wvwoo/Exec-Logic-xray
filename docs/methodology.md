# Methodology | المنهجية المرحلية

## Scope
The intended record key is **association registration number × financial year**. The planned years are 2023–2025. Out-of-period rows and projected budgets must not be presented as actual financial statements.

## Sources and identity
Use official nonprofit registration records and source-specific published financial statements. Retain the original evidence, accounting period, currency/units, PDF page, file hash, and extraction provenance in a **restricted audit workspace**, subject to publication permissions.

## Deduplication
Keep one record per license and financial year. Resolve financial conflicts against original source evidence, never solely by picking the newest file.

## Controlled exclusions
- Exclude budget projections erroneously labeled as actual financial statements and out-of-scope years.
- Quarantine wrong-entity, contradictory or otherwise unsubstantiated records.
- Preserve every excluded record in a traceable private log of reasons and source locations.
- Require independent human review and project-lead approval before calling an included record independently certified.

## Financial integrity
Check assets = liabilities + net assets; compare income/expense movements and net assets changes, with exceptions documented against original source. Arithmetic equality is a necessary check but **not** source authentication.

## Sample, weighting and inference
Fix registry, sample, geographic grouping, analysis units and weights *before* calculation. Do not re-label results from a previous version as v6. Investigate response/non-disclosure bias and run sensitivity analyses.

## Limits
A financial alert is an exploratory monitoring aid, not a validated prediction of insolvency and not an accusation toward any association.

**Status:** This is a specification of intended methods, not proof that the final audit or statistical reproduction is complete.
